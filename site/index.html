Symbolic weight

Each word scores on how many riddles and sayings use it, how many cultures share it, and how many other meanings it connects to. The words at the top are the ones people across the world keep coming back to, and the ones that carry the most weight when you say them to someone.

    How it works

    The score

    weight = uses + 2 × cultures + ½ × links, scaled so the top word is 100. Spread across cultures counts double, because a word that matters in many places says more about people than a word one tradition repeats.

    The data

    Texts are short retellings, not translations. Each one is tagged by hand with the meanings it touches.

    Symbols

    The top words get a traditional sign: alchemical elements, planet signs, the ankh, the ensō. They are Unicode characters drawn with Google's Noto symbol fonts.

    Opportunities

    The mind map's “opportunities” are starting points for brainstorming, not market research: what a word tends to make people feel, and where that feeling is already sold.
